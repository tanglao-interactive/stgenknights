3rd Degree Ceremonial Team

Welcoming brother Knights through the Exemplification of Charity, Unity and Fraternity.

What this means for our Council

The exemplification welcomes candidates as Third Degree members and helps First and Second Degree Knights advance. It teaches charity, unity, and fraternity, inviting members to live their Catholic faith and support their brother Knights, families, parish, and council.

Our ceremonial team supports the presentation and candidate welcome. CUF stands for Charity, Unity, and Fraternity. The Fourth Degree roster is listed separately; this exemplification confers Third Degree membership.
